About Plant Care AI

Empowering gardeners, farmers, and plant lovers with instant computer vision disease detection.

Our Mission

Deep Learning Core

Beyond detection, our expert Knowledge Engine generates actionable treatment plans covering chemical, organic, and cultural practices.
